Early Bird & Promos

Extend the Early Bird deadline, end it early, or put it back to the default. Changes go live on clmits.com, checkout and payment reminders immediately. All times are Manila time; a new date closes at 11:59 PM that day.

Promos keep an option's total and only change how it's paid: a down payment today, then the rest split into equal monthly installments. Use Run again on a past promo to bring it back.

People who already registered keep the price they signed up at. Where Standard Rate waits for Early Bird (Cloud + DE), it opens on its own once Early Bird closes.
