Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

CL Magno I.T. Solutions ("we," "us," "our") provides application development services, live bootcamps, self-paced training, companion books, and corporate I.T. training through clmits.com and portal.clmits.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Inquiry form. When you submit the contact form on clmits.com, your name, email address, selected interest, and message are sent to us via Formspree.
  • Bootcamp registration. When you reserve a slot in a live bootcamp, we collect your full name, email, phone number, and the pricing tier you selected. Payment is processed by PayMongo — we never see or store your card, GCash, or Maya credentials directly.
  • Student portal account. Creating a portal account collects your email, full name, phone (if provided), and a securely hashed password (we cannot read your password). We also store your course progress, quiz attempts, and any messages you send through the portal's comment feature.

How We Use It

We use your information to deliver the service you signed up for: responding to inquiries, registering you for a cohort and sending your schedule and login details, processing payments, tracking your progress through course material, and communicating with you about your enrollment. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who We Share It With

We work with a small number of service providers to run the business, each of whom only receives what they need to do their job:

  • Formspree — delivers contact form submissions to our inbox.
  • PayMongo — processes bootcamp payments (GCash, Maya, card, QR Ph).
  • Netlify — hosts the website and student portal.
  • Neon — hosts the database behind the student portal.

We do not share your information with anyone else except where required by law.

Cookies

The student portal sets a single secure, HttpOnly session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the services you've enrolled in, and as required for tax and accounting records. You can request deletion of your account and personal data at any time — see "Your Rights" below.

Your Rights

You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account entirely. Email admin@clmits.com and we'll action it within a reasonable time.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults and working professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services evolve. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Questions?

Reach us at admin@clmits.com.
